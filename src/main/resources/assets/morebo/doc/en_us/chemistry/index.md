# Chemistry Book

Welcome to the **Chemical Synthesis** manual! This book covers the apparatus, recipes and chemical substances.

## Apparatus

- ![Test Tube](item:morebo:test_tube) **Test Tube**: The basic tool for chemical synthesis.
  - Hold the tube and right-click with a **bucket / potion** in the other hand → stores a **solution**;
  - Right-click with any other item → stores it as a **solid**;
  - **Sneak + right-click** → removes contents (solutions are poured out as potions);
  - When the contents match a recipe, synthesis happens automatically.

## Recipes (defined by datapack)

Add the required materials to the tube and it synthesizes automatically (recipes marked "needs solution" first require a solution):

- **Aphrodisiac (sm)**: Pink Dye + Sugar + Golden Carrot (needs solution) → ![Aphrodisiac](item:morebo:sm) ×16
- **Growth Hormone (ghw)**: Golden Carrot + Glistering Melon Slice + Redstone (needs solution) → ![Growth Hormone](item:morebo:ghw) ×32
- **RE-X (rex)**: Iron Ingot + Coal + Gunpowder + Blaze Powder (no solution) → ![RE-X](item:morebo:rex) ×9

## Chemicals

- ![RE-X](item:morebo:rex) **RE-X**: A rare substance produced by chemical synthesis (see recipes above). Recipes and outputs are loaded dynamically from the datapack `data/morebo/chemistry_recipes/`.
