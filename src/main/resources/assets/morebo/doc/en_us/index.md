# MoreBreedingOptimize

Welcome to **MoreBreedingOptimize**! This manual introduces the mod's main items and gameplay.

## Main Features

- **Breeding with End Rods**: Right-click an animal with an End Rod to trigger breeding; quickly sneaking on an End Rod may trigger drops (probability configurable).
- **Chemistry Synthesis**: Use test tubes, beakers and petri dishes to synthesize potions and reagents.

## Item List

- **Aphrodisiac (sm)**: A potion that puts animals into **heat**.
- **Aphrodisiac Syringe (smt)**: Injects the loaded aphrodisiac into an animal.
- **Empty Syringe (nullsyringe)**: An empty syringe, used to load reagents.
- **Growth Hormone (ghw)** / **Growth Hormone Syringe (gh)**: Promotes animal growth.
- **Blood Draw Syringe (bts)**: Draws a blood sample from an animal.
- **Cupid's Bow (lovebow)**: Shoots arrows of love that trigger kiss events.
- **Wild Dog Milk (dogm)**: A special drink.
- **Raw Cola (jnhc)**: Grants the **Benzhou Enhancement** effect; while active, type `@滨州 <your message>` in chat to talk to Benzhou.
- **MoreBreedingOptimize Manual (sbook)**: The book you are reading right now.

## Effect List

- **Lust (lust)**: Puts animals into heat for breeding.
- **Eternal (eternal)**: A persistent beneficial effect.
- **Benzhou Enhancement (bzstronger)**: Granted after drinking Raw Cola; generates a light above your head and unlocks chat dialogue with Benzhou.

## Configuration

Thrust strength, drop chances, sudden death chances, kiss detection range and more can be adjusted in `morebo-common.toml`.
